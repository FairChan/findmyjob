## 5. Student users

### 5.1 Free to use

FindMYJob is free for students. There are currently no paid features for students; if we introduce any, we will update these terms and tell you in advance.

### 5.2 Profile and verification

- Your profile, experience and resume must be truthful, and you must not pretend to be someone else.
- School verification may only use your own school email; one school email can verify only one account.
- You can apply for jobs once your profile completeness reaches 60 points (may change).
- Whether to join the talent pool is your choice; it is off by default. Who can see your data is explained in section 6 of the Privacy Policy.

### 5.3 Job-viewing quota

- Visitors can only see the job list; you must sign in to see job details.
- After signing in you can open a limited number of job details: currently 20 to start with, 10 more for every 3 qualified referrals, and no limit after 12 qualified referrals in total.
- Only actively opening the details of a job you have not opened before counts as 1. Viewing the same job again does not count; jobs you have applied for and jobs a company invited you to apply for do not count.
- Once your quota is used up, new jobs show only the title, company and state.
- We may change these numbers or remove the quota; the quota page shows the current values. Our staff may also add quota for you for a good reason.

### 5.4 Inviting classmates (qualified referrals)

- A classmate who signs up through your invite link counts as 1 qualified referral only after their email is verified and their profile completeness reaches 90 points (may change; we may also require their school to be verified).
- Each invitee counts for one referrer only. Inviting yourself does not count, including different spellings of the same email (for example a Gmail address with added dots or a "+tag").
- At most 3 referrals per person can qualify each day; any extra stay "pending" and are re-checked automatically the next day.
- If an invitee later deletes their account, the quota you already received is not taken back.
- You must not obtain referrals through fake accounts, bulk sign-ups, mutual referral swapping, paying people to sign up, spam or similar methods. If we find this, we may void the referrals concerned and recalculate your quota immediately, and may suspend the accounts involved.
- Your quota page shows each invitee's masked name, profile completeness and referral status.

### 5.5 Applications and invitations to apply

- Applying for a job means you agree that the company can see your full name, contact details and the resume you chose; the company does not spend credits.
- You can apply for the same job only once, and you can withdraw. Withdrawing does not take back the access the company already has; only blocking the company or deleting your account stops the platform showing it your data (see 6.4 of the Privacy Policy).
- Accepting a company's invitation to apply counts as applying; you can also decline. Invitations not answered within 14 days expire automatically.
- Cover notes are limited to 500 characters.

## 6. Employer users

### 6.1 Company verification

- To apply for verification you submit: legal name, display names, SSM registration number, industry, size, website, address and state, description, logo, and verification documents (SSM certificate or business licence; PDF / PNG / JPG; up to 10MB each; up to 5 files).
- We review manually and verify by calling back through public channels (SSM records, official website, public phone number), not the contact details in your documents. We may ask for more documents.
- The result is approval or rejection. A rejection comes with a reason, and you can correct and resubmit.
- You promise that what you submit is true, lawful and up to date, and you will update it promptly when it changes (for example the company name or registration status).
- Verification only means we have checked your registration details. It is not a guarantee of the company's business, jobs or conduct.
- Company positioning tags on company pages (for example "multinational") are set only by us; companies cannot set them.

### 6.2 Team

- OWNERs and ADMINs can invite members by email. An invite link is valid for 7 days, can be used once, and can only be accepted by the invited email address.
- During the pilot each company can have at most 3 members; in the paid phase this depends on the plan.
- A removed member loses access at their next action.

### 6.3 Credits (pilot)

- Companies pay nothing during the pilot.
- After verification, we top your credits up to the monthly amount (currently 30 credits), and top them up again on the 1st of each month. This is a top-up, not an addition; unused credits from last month do not add up.
- Unlocking a student costs credits (currently 1 credit). A company needs to unlock the same student only once, and the unlock is shared within the company; repeat unlocks cost nothing. When a student applies for your job, you can see their details without spending credits.
- If your balance runs out, wait for the top-up on the 1st of next month.
- Credits have no cash value: they cannot be exchanged for cash, transferred, sold or used outside FindMYJob. Pilot credits are not refundable or exchangeable.
- If a student later blocks your company, is suspended or deletes their account, you can no longer see their data on the platform, and credits already spent are not refunded.
- We may change the monthly credits, the credits needed per unlock and related limits; the values shown on the pages apply. If we find credits being misused, we may take back or freeze them.
- Invitations to apply cost no credits but use the monthly invitation quota (currently 20 per month during the pilot).

### 6.4 Moving to paid

- After the pilot, FindMYJob will charge companies; credits and plans will need to be bought (bank transfer or online payment).
- We will give pilot companies at least 2 weeks' notice before charging starts.
- Prices, plan contents, payment methods, invoices, refunds, credit validity, and what happens to remaining pilot credits will be set out in paid terms published at that time. 【TBD T18: paid-phase terms】
- A company that does not accept the paid terms can stop using FindMYJob and close its company account. 【TBD T11: how a company closes its account and deletes its data, and how long the data is kept】

### 6.5 Rules for using the talent pool and student data

Student data includes the full names, contact details, resumes and profile content you obtain through search, unlocks, applications and invitations. You must:

- Use the talent pool and student data only for your company's genuine recruitment.
- Not sell or rent student data, or give it to any third party, unless the student agrees.
- Not use student data for sales, marketing, loans, insurance, paid training, religious or political promotion, or any other purpose unrelated to recruitment.
- Not scrape, bulk-download, export or use software to access the talent pool automatically, and not try to get around the daily limits (currently, per member per day: up to 200 profile views, 300 pages of search results and 50 unlocks; per company per day: up to 400 profile views in total).
- Not remove or cover the watermark on resumes, and not use anonymised profiles to work out who a student is and contact them outside the platform without unlocking.
- Keep student data secure and delete it when recruitment ends or it is no longer needed; stop contacting a student immediately when they ask you to.
- Take responsibility yourself, under the PDPA, for student data you take off the platform. 【TBD T28: whether the employer is an independent data controller for this data, and whether the platform and employers need a separate agreement】

Please note: students can see your company name and the time in "Who viewed me" and "Who unlocked me", and every page of a resume you download carries a watermark with your company name, the date and the operator ID.

## 7. Job posting rules

- Post only jobs that genuinely exist at your company and that you are actually recruiting for. Do not post fake jobs, jobs that were filled long ago, or jobs meant only to collect resumes. 【TBD T22: whether posting on behalf of clients is allowed】
- Fill in the job title, duties, work location (state / city), work mode, salary range, number of openings, closing date, and language and skill requirements truthfully. You may choose not to show the salary, but you must not misstate it.
- The "accepts student pass holders" and "can help with a work pass" options must be ticked truthfully. You must comply with Malaysian employment and immigration law and must not place a candidate in work their visa or pass does not allow.
- Do not charge candidates anything under any name, for example application fees, deposits, training fees, medical check fees, uniform fees or permit fees, and do not require candidates to buy products or take loans.
- Do not ask candidates to hand over original passports or identity documents, bank cards, online-banking details or verification codes.
- Do not post jobs that are illegal, sexual, gambling-related, fraudulent, pyramid or recruit-to-earn schemes, receiving and passing on transfers ("money mule" work), fake-order "task" work or similar.
- 【TBD T23: whether jobs located outside Malaysia are allowed】 Do not use high pay to lure candidates abroad when the employer, duties or location are unclear.
- During the pilot every job is reviewed manually by us before it goes public. If you change the title, description, requirements, benefits, salary or location of a published job, it goes back for review and is taken offline during review.
- We may reject, take offline or remove jobs that break these rules. In the paid phase the number of jobs may be limited by plan.

## 8. What no user may do

- Pretend to be another person, another company or FindMYJob; sign up or apply for verification with false information.
- Defraud, harass, threaten or intimidate other users.
- Ask anyone for money, identity documents, bank details or verification codes, on FindMYJob or after meeting them through FindMYJob.
- Send malicious files, phishing links or malicious code.
- Get around human verification, rate limits, viewing quotas, the sign-up cap or other security measures; attack, scan or interfere with our systems; access the site with bots or scrapers.
- Create multiple accounts, share accounts, or game referrals.
- Make reports in bad faith.
- Do anything that breaks Malaysian law.

**Anti-scam reminder for students**: genuine employers do not ask you to pay first and do not keep your passport. If a job looks suspicious, tap "Report" on the job page; in an emergency, call the Malaysian police on 999.

## 9. Reports and how we handle them

- When handling a report, we may take the job offline, ask the company to explain, suspend or terminate the company account, and where necessary report to the authorities as the law provides.
- We handle reports as quickly as we can but do not promise a specific time.
